# Code of Conduct

This org is the online half of a four-week residency where people share a building. The
same conduct applies in both places.

**Expected:** treat disagreement as technical, credit people's work, assume the person
opposite you knows something you don't, and take "no" as an answer the first time.

**Not tolerated:** harassment of any kind, sexualised attention, sustained disruption,
publishing anyone's private information, or deliberately undermining someone's work.

**Reporting:** speak to any steward at the Commons Hub, or email the organisers. Reports
stay with the people handling them. Stewards can remove content, revoke org access, and
ask someone to leave the residency.

Being technically right is not a defence. Being new is not a disqualification.
